# 🎥 DevScope – 3 to 5 Minute Walkthrough Video Script & Demo Guide

This script is engineered for recording a project demonstration video or presenting **DevScope** during Google Data Analytics Apprenticeship interviews.

---

## ⏱️ Video Breakdown (Total Duration: 4 Minutes)

| Segment | Duration | Target Screen / Visual | Key Talking Points |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Executive Introduction** | 0:00 – 0:45 | Dashboard Top & Executive Summary Banner | Project Purpose, Multi-Source Datasets (SO 2021-24, Job Postings, Octoverse), Stakeholder Business Problem |
| **2. Live Dashboard Demo** | 0:45 – 2:15 | Tabs 1, 5, & 9 (Executive Overview, Map, Tech Search) | Interactive Filters, Global Map Tooltips, Instant Tech Search & Side-by-Side Comparison |

---

## 📜 Full Presentation Script

### 🎙️ Segment 1: Executive Introduction (0:00 – 0:45)
> *"Hello! I'm excited to present **DevScope – Developer Career Intelligence Platform**, a production-grade analytics platform built for global workforce planning and talent acquisition leadership.*
> 
> *Engineering executives today face a major decision problem: salary inflation, shifting remote policies, rapid AI tool adoption, and talent shortages. To solve this, DevScope synthesizes over **9,000 authentic Stack Overflow survey responses spanning 2021 to 2024**, combined with **GitHub Octoverse data** and **1,500 real-world tech job market postings**.*
> 
> *Crucially, every dashboard view, query, and report answers **'So What?'** with explicit business insights, implications, and recommendations."*

---

### 🎙️ Segment 2: Interactive Product Dashboard Demo (0:45 – 2:15)
> *(Navigate to `http://localhost:8501`)*
> 
> *"At the top of the platform, we feature a concise **Executive Summary** highlighting our core macro findings. Below, our **Global Sidebar Filters** allow executives to instantly slice data by Survey Year, Country, Experience Level, and Work Mode.*
> 
> *Let's look at **Tab 5: Global Talent Map**. Notice how hovering over any country—like Germany or the United States—reveals rich metrics: Developer Count, Median Salary, AI Adoption Rate, Remote Share, and Average Experience.*
> 
> *Now let's switch to **Tab 9: Technology Benchmark & Instant Search**. An engineering manager can type or select any technology—such as **Rust** vs **Python**—and instantly compare median compensation ($138k vs $115k), AI tool usage rate, 3-year CAGR momentum (+28.6%), and remote work prevalence. This equips talent teams to make data-backed hiring decisions."*

---

### 🎙️ Segment 3: Technical Architecture & SQL Engine (2:15 – 3:15)
> *(Show `README.md` architecture diagram & SQL query files)*
> 
> *"Behind the scenes, DevScope follows enterprise software standards. Our ETL pipeline handles extraction, schema validation, missingness handling based on our documented Data Dictionary, and loading into a relational star schema.*
> 
> *We wrote over 50 consolidated SQL analytics queries covering compensation drivers, cloud adoption, career mobility, and recruitment bottlenecks. Everything is tested using **19 automated pytest integration tests** with 100% pass rates, and fully dockerized for single-command deployment."*

---

### 🎙️ Segment 4: Measured Business Impact & Conclusion (3:15 – 4:00)
> *(Show `reports/executive_report.pdf` or `docs/case_study.md`)*
> 
> *"Rather than making unverified claims, DevScope uses calibrated analyst language. For instance, based on our 4-year trend analysis, we recommend a 'Remote-First for Senior Engineers, Hybrid for Entry-Level' strategy—which may support improved developer retention and candidate reach.*
> 
> *DevScope transforms raw survey rows into strategic decision support. Thank you for your time!"*

---

## 💡 Tips for Recording
1. **Resolution**: Record in 1080p full screen with dark browser theme.
2. **Audio**: Use a clear microphone with steady pacing.
3. **Cursor Highlights**: Use mouse highlights when pointing out sidebar filters and map tooltips.
